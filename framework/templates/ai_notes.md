## What AI may do

AI has three jobs on this project, and no others:

1. Brainstorm questions to research.
2. Suggest search terms.
3. Tidy and organize your own notes.

## What AI may not do

<!-- density-exempt: X, not Y -- the may-not list the batch 1 brief's entry for this form requires: never the only source, never the child's recommendation, never the adults' decisions -->

1. Be your only source.
2. Write your recommendation. The recommendation is your work, even when AI helped you organize the notes behind it.
3. Decide anything about passports, entry, visas, safety, medical questions, medication, bookings, payments, legal requirements, or the final budget. Those are for the adults.

If AI gave you a fact you want to use, verify it with a non-AI source or take it out. For a major recommendation, use at least two non-AI sources. AI may help you brainstorm or organize, and it is never the source of a fact.

## The privacy rule

**Never put family or personal details into an AI tool.** No names, no addresses, no dates, no booking details.

**This includes photos and scans of your filled-in worksheets and binder pages.** One filled page carries the whole roster and the plan in a single shot. If you want AI's help with your work, a grown-up retypes the question without the personal parts.

AI conversations may be stored by the company that runs the tool, so treat them as not private.
