# Language and Etiquette Quick Sheet

**Status: Conditional core** -- becomes Core if your family wants the language and etiquette quick sheet in the binder. This one is worth keeping, because you'll use this sheet on the trip itself.

Build this **for your own pocket**, as a tool you'll use on the trip, not just a binder page. You'll say hello and thank you with it, order food, and read signs while you're standing there. Make it small enough to carry, or photograph it on a phone.

Use this page in Session 47. Open this session's Destination Notes and fill this in from there. The words themselves live in the pack, because they're different everywhere.

Ask a grown-up to check the wording before you rely on it, and write the date you checked.

Six good lines you'll actually use are plenty.

Every place has its own normal way of doing things. There's a reason behind each one. You're learning how things are done here. It isn't a test. Nobody expects a visitor to get it perfect, and people are glad you tried.

If the bathing or shared-facility question comes up, that one's a grown-up's call about what's right for you, as [Session 47](../sessions/phase_08_readiness_final/47_language_and_etiquette.md) says. Ask them.
