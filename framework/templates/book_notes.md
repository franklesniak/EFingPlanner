# Book Notes

Use this page in Session 06, with one long source: a guidebook, or one reputable travel website. Fill in the block that matches what you used, and leave the other one blank. Then write down three places that sound interesting and why. Copy this page again for the next source.

**Write everything in your own words.** Do not copy the source's sentences onto your page. A short line you quote for a citation is fine, but a page of somebody else's writing teaches you nothing and is not yours to copy.
