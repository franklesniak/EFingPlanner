# Family Interview

Use this page to interview the travelers on your trip. Ask the three questions below, write the answers down, and record each person by relationship or role rather than by name. That keeps private details off the page. One page holds several short interviews, so you don't need a fresh copy per person.

## The three questions to ask

1. What would you love on this trip?
2. What do you prefer: busy days or slow ones, big cities or quiet places, planned time or free time?
3. What might tire you out?

If you only have time for one, ask this one: *"What is one thing you'd love on this trip?"*

## If someone is hard to reach

Ask them by text or call. Or have a grown-up ask and bring back the answer. Or interview whoever is around now and mark the rest "asked through a grown-up." You can also leave an answer open. Do not wait on anyone's schedule.
