# Session 31: Route Trade-Off Report

You are here: Phase 5 (Route, Length, and Transportation). Not a First Taste step. Previous: [30 Trains, Transit, and Travel Cards](30_trains_transit_and_travel_cards.md) | Next: [32 Checkpoint 4 Route and Trip Length](32_checkpoint_4_route_and_trip_length.md)

**For parents:**

- Status: Core
- Planner skill: making trade-offs
- Estimated time: 20-30 minutes
- Parent involvement: parent review after session
- Materials: a fresh [Trade-Off Report](../../templates/tradeoff_report.md) page, your route map notes from Session 28, your nights table from Session 29, your transportation notes from Session 30, your family's [current travel assumptions](../../templates/current_family_travel_assumptions.md) page (for the budget band), your [Source Log](../../templates/source_log.md)

## Goal

Compare two routes for the trip, and recommend one.

## Start Here

On a fresh Trade-Off Report page, write "Which route should we take?" as the decision you're making. That's your start.

## Steps

This is your route trade-off, one of the three trade-off reports you'll write in the project. The table on your Trade-Off Report page is already drawn, with an example row at the top. You plug in your own two routes.

1. **Choose two routes to compare.** Route 1 can be the plan you have now. Route 2 should be a different shape. One classic pair is fewer cities with more nights in each, against more cities, seen faster. On a one-base trip, compare two ways to use your base: fewer must-dos with more free time, against more must-dos, seen faster. Use the base-to-must-do times from Session 28.
2. **Put one route in each column.** Route 1 goes under Option A and Route 2 under Option B. Write which cities each route includes. The example row shows the shape: "Route 1: City A + City B" and "Route 2: City A + City C".
3. **Fill every row for both routes.** Go down the page: pros, cons, cost effect, time effect, energy effect, and what you'd miss. For cost, write higher, about the same, or lower. You don't need prices.
4. **Use travel times from Directions.** Copy them from your route map notes. They came from the map's Directions in Session 28, so they're better than a guess from the map picture.
5. **Do the budget-band check.** In that row, ask: "Does this route still fit our rough budget band?" More cities and more hotel moves usually cost more.
6. **Fill the source rows.** Where did your facts come from? Use your Source Log.
7. **Write your closing lines**: your recommendation, two or three reasons, what the family gives up by choosing it, and what a grown-up still needs to check or decide.

If your table gets too wide to print, split it. Put Route 1 and Route 2 on the first page and the closing lines on the next.

## Workspace

Use your Trade-Off Report page and your Source Log.

You can say your answers to an adult who writes them, or draw them, if that's easier.

## Artifact Created

Your Trade-Off Report, comparing at least two route options, with your recommendation.

## Stop Point

You are done when both routes have every row filled in, you've written which one you recommend, and you've named what the family gives up by choosing it. "Not sure yet" counts as an answer in any row.

## Source Check

Your travel times came from the map you used in Session 28, and that map is already in your Source Log. List it in the report's source rows. If you checked any new times today, log the map again with the Map form on your [simple citation forms](../../templates/simple_citation.md) page: map tool, place or route searched, and date I checked it. Travel times can change, so mark them for a grown-up to check again before booking.

## Finish and Quality Check

Finished? Use the [Finish and Quality Check card](../../student_guide/finish_and_quality_check.md) in your student guide.

## If You Get Stuck

Stuck? Use the [When I'm Stuck card](../../student_guide/when_im_stuck.md) in your student guide.

## Optional Extension

If you have extra energy, give each row your own importance weight, from 1 to 3, and argue the trade-off in a short paragraph. If you'd like to score the routes, the [scoring rubric](../../templates/scoring_rubric.md) has a lighter three-question version beside the full one, and either is fine. If not, your table is a complete report.

## Parent Notes

This is the city and route trade-off, one of the three trade-off reports the project asks for. Review it after the session.

Then ask one quick spoken question: "Walk me through how you weighed this option against that one." Treat it as a chat about how they thought it through. If your child can't yet show how they weighed the two, give more support before Phase 6: talk through one row together, then let them do the next.

The shape of the journey there and back, such as whether you arrive and leave from the same place, stays with you. Your child works only with the arrival and departure places you've written down, and moves a city block if those change.
