# Session 54: After You Get Back

You are here: Phase 9 (After You Get Back). Not a First Taste step. Previous: [53 Reflection and Handoff](../phase_08_readiness_final/53_reflection_and_handoff.md) | Next: none

**For parents:**

- Status: Optional -- after the trip
- Planner skill: planning realistic time; revising a plan
- Estimated time: one sitting of 15-20 minutes
- Parent involvement: 5-minute check-in; you share the trip numbers you choose, and your child does the comparing
- Materials: your [in-trip capture card](../../trip_starter/in_trip_capture_card.md), if your family kept it; your final reflection from Session 53; your budget pages (your Session 33 estimate, and your Session 39 budget summary on the Core path); your day cards or itinerary, if you made them; and whatever numbers from the trip a grown-up chooses to share

(For an at-a-glance version, see the matching entry in [the parent session notes](../../parent_guide/session_support_notes.md); the Parent Notes below have the full detail.)

## Goal

This session is optional, and it comes after the trip. Compare a few days of your plan with what happened, and write one lesson for next time. Skipping it is fine, and everything you made before the trip stays as it is.

## Start Here

Find your in-trip capture card and read one line of it. That's your start.

If you skipped the card, read one line of your Session 53 final reflection instead.

## Steps

You can do this weeks or months after you get home. Short answers are fine.

1. Pick two or three days of the trip. Your capture card or your day cards can help you choose, or pick days you remember well.
2. For each day, fill in one table in the Workspace. Compare what you planned with what happened: time, cost, energy and pacing (how tired you got, and how full the day felt), crowds, and how much you enjoyed it. A grown-up tells you the cost numbers they want to share, and you do the comparing. Money from the trip is the grown-ups' to share or keep private, so the Cost row can stay empty.
3. Look back at the guesses you checked in Session 53: your budget check, and any time or price guesses you made. How close did they come to what happened on the trip? Being off is normal, and noticing it is the whole point. (See your [planner mindset card](../../student_guide/planner_mindset.md).)
4. Answer the three questions in the Workspace: what did your plan get right, what did it get wrong, and what would you change next time? Your guesses from step 3 are part of your plan too.
5. Write one line for your binder: the one lesson you want to keep for a future trip.

You are done when you have compared a few days and written your one lesson. Then file this page at the back of your binder or folder, after your final reflection.

If you have extra energy, compare another day or two. Or write a short note to a future planner, maybe you on your next trip: what would you tell them? If not, you are done.

## Parent Notes

**Optional, after the trip, and fully skippable.** This page takes the habit of guessing and then checking one step further, to what the trip cost and how the days went. Your child practiced that habit during the project, with whichever guesses they made: time, price, travel time and the budget check. So skipping this page loses nothing. Do it only if your family wants to, weeks or months after you get home.

**What you share.** You decide which numbers from the trip to share, such as a daily food total, a ticket or a ride. Your child does the comparing. Treat each gap as information for next time. Keep confirmation numbers and payment details off the page, as the [privacy and safety page](../../docs/privacy_and_safety.md) says.

**When the plan changed on the trip.** If a day went differently from the plan, the [coaching guide](../../parent_guide/coaching_and_support.md) has a short script for that moment, and its words fit here too.
