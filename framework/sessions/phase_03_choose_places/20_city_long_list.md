# Session 20: City Long-List

You are here: Phase 3 (Choose Places). Not a First Taste step. Previous: [19 Other Places Research](19_other_places_research.md) | Next: [21 Compare Cities](21_compare_cities.md)

**For parents:**

- Status: Core
- Planner skill: organizing information
- Estimated time: 20-30 minutes
- Parent involvement: none / independent work
- Materials: all your City Research Cards, your [Source Log](../../templates/source_log.md), a blank [City Long-List](../../templates/city_long_list.md) page

## Goal

Put every place you researched onto one long-list, so you can see them all at once.

## Start Here

Write the name of your first City Research Card's place in the first row of your City Long-List. That's your start.

## Steps

You've made a card for every place you looked into. Now you gather them onto one page. This session needs no new research. Everything you need is already on your cards and in your Source Log.

Your [City Long-List](../../templates/city_long_list.md) page has five columns. Fill one row per place:

1. **Place**: the name from the top of the card.
2. **Why it caught my attention**: one short reason, in your own words.
3. **One memorable fact**: copy it from the card.
4. **Source**: where that fact came from. Find it in your Source Log.
5. **Keep researching?** Circle yes, no, or maybe.

Go card by card until every place has a row. Your deep-dive cities go on the list too.

**Five to eight places is the shape to aim for.** That's a target, and you don't have to hit it. If you researched four places, list four. If you researched ten, list all ten. Every place you researched belongs on the list, even one you already feel "no" about. Writing down why you said no is useful later.

## Workspace

Use your City Long-List page, with your City Research Cards and Source Log beside it.

You can say your answers to an adult who writes them, or draw them, if that's easier.

## Artifact Created

Your City long-list: every place you researched, usually five to eight, each with a reason, a fact, a source, and a keep-researching mark.

## Stop Point

You are done when every place you researched has a row, with a yes, no, or maybe mark. "Maybe" is a finished answer, and so is "not sure yet" in any other box. You don't need to find new places to fill empty rows.

## Source Check

No new lookups today. The Source column comes straight from your Source Log. If you can't find where a fact came from, write "not sure" in that box and ask a grown-up whether it needs checking again.

## Finish and Quality Check

Finished? Use the [Finish and Quality Check card](../../student_guide/finish_and_quality_check.md) in your student guide.

## If You Get Stuck

Stuck? Use the [When I'm Stuck card](../../student_guide/when_im_stuck.md) in your student guide.

## Optional Extension

If you have extra energy, draw a small star next to the two or three places you're most excited about. They're a good place to start in Session 21. If not, you are done.

## Parent Notes

The long-list is binder evidence, so it gets its own page. The five-to-eight range is a target to aim for; if your child researched fewer places, the list is still complete. A "no" with a reason is a good row, because it shows a choice was made. Next session, the comparison takes the "yes" and "maybe" places forward.
