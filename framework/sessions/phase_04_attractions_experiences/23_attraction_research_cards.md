# Session 23: Attraction Research Cards

You are here: Phase 4 (Attractions and Experiences). Not a First Taste step. Previous: [22 Checkpoint 2 City Shortlist](../phase_03_choose_places/22_checkpoint_2_city_shortlist.md) | Next: [24 Culture, History, Nature, Food, and Fun Balance](24_culture_history_nature_food_fun_balance.md)

**For parents:**

- Status: Core
- Planner skill: checking sources
- Estimated time: 20-30 minutes (can be several sittings)
- Parent involvement: none / independent work
- Materials: this session's Destination Notes, a device with the kid-safe filter on, blank [Attraction Research Cards](../../templates/attraction_research_card.md), your City Research Cards with their starred sights, your "things I can't wait to see" page, your [Source Log](../../templates/source_log.md)

## Goal

Start a card for each thing you most want to do on the trip.

## Start Here

Pick one starred sight from your City Research Cards. Write its name in the Name row of a blank Attraction Research Card. That's your start.

## Steps

Your cities are shortlisted, so now you look at what you'd do in them. One Attraction Research Card holds one place or experience. It keeps that place's facts together, so later you can compare fairly, with the facts in front of you.

1. **Start with your stars.** The sights you starred on your City Research Cards back in Session 15 and the deep dives are your first ideas.
2. **Open this session's Destination Notes** for more starter ideas. You'll find big, well-known attractions mixed with everyday ones that cost little. Each one is an idea to research, and the choosing is yours.
3. **Fill in the card, row by row.** The [Attraction Research Card](../../templates/attraction_research_card.md) has every row you need, in order. Rough answers are fine, like "about two hours" for time needed.
4. **Guess the price first.** Before you look up a ticket price, write your guess in the card's row for it: "My guess at the ticket price, before I looked it up." Then check the attraction's official website. Write what you found in the next row, with the date. How close was your guess? Being off is normal, and your [planner mindset card](../../student_guide/planner_mindset.md) says why that's fine.
5. **Look for one downside.** Crowds, a long line, lots of walking, or a high price all count.
6. **Fill the planning assumption rows** at the bottom, the same way you did on your city cards.
7. **Feed your "things I can't wait to see" page.** If a card gets you excited, add that attraction to the page you started in Session 01. It's the one page for that feeling, so keep it all there.

**Two cards is a good day's work.** The whole set is ten cards, and you can take as many sittings as you need to get there. Come back to this session until you have ten, and then move on. Session 24 checks the mix of all ten, so it helps to have them all first.

## Workspace

Use one Attraction Research Card per place or experience, and your Source Log.

You can say your answers to an adult who writes them, or draw them, if that's easier.

## Artifact Created

Your attraction cards: one card per thing you want to do, each with a reason, a rough time, a downside, and a source.

## Stop Point

You are done for today when you have at least two cards with a reason, a rough time needed, one downside, and a source each. More cards can wait for another sitting. Your attraction set is finished at ten cards, however many sittings that takes. Come back to this session until you reach ten, before Session 24, so every card gets its balance check. "Ask an adult" and "not sure yet" are fine answers on any card.

## Source Check

Log this session's Destination Notes (title and today's date) and every source you used. For a ticket price, the attraction's own official website is the best source, and the date you checked matters as much as the number, because prices change. Mark each price for a grown-up to check again before booking.

## Finish and Quality Check

Finished? Use the [Finish and Quality Check card](../../student_guide/finish_and_quality_check.md) in your student guide.

## If You Get Stuck

Stuck? Use the [When I'm Stuck card](../../student_guide/when_im_stuck.md) in your student guide.

## Optional Extension

If you have extra energy, look at your price guesses so far. Were most of them too high, too low, or close? Write one line about what you noticed. If not, you are done.

## Parent Notes

The price guess is practice, and a wrong guess tends to make the checked number stick. Leave the guess as it is, and ask what surprised them.

Ten cards is the Core floor for attractions, and reaching it across several sittings is the expected pace. Two cards in a sitting is a finished day. The "things I can't wait to see" page stays the one place for excitement, and attractions go there too.

Prices and hours your child records are dated snapshots. Treat each one as a note to check again before anyone books.
