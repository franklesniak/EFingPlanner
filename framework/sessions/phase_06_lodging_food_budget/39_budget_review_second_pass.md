# Session 39: Budget Review, Second Pass

You are here: Phase 6 (Lodging, Food, and Budget). Not a First Taste step. Previous: [38 Daily Cost Estimates](38_daily_cost_estimates.md) | Next: [40 Realistic Day Planning](../phase_07_itinerary_building/40_realistic_day_planning.md)

**For parents:**

- Status: Core
- Planner skill: revising a plan
- Estimated time: 20-30 minutes
- Parent involvement: parent review after session; beforehand, tell your child how many rooms each overnight city needs
- Materials: your [Budget Estimate page](../../templates/budget_estimate.md) from Session 33 and a blank one for this pass, your daily cost table from Session 38, your Hotel Comparison Cards, your route and nights from Checkpoint 4, your route map notes from Session 28, the City Research Card for any region on your route (for its town), your My Calls page (for your special pick), your attraction cards and your [When the Plan Changes card](../../student_guide/when_the_plan_changes.md) (if your special pick hits a block, or a grown-up agrees to a change), your part-trip note from Session 33 (if you made one) or the grown-up's answer on who is there on which days, your Session 29 formula (for its "- 1 flight day" marks), or a grown-up who can tell you which flights land on a later date, your [Trip-Basics card](../../templates/trip_basics.md) (for the number of travelers), your family's [current travel assumptions](../../templates/current_family_travel_assumptions.md) page (for the budget band), your [Source Log](../../templates/source_log.md), a calculator (allowed)

## Goal

Update your budget to match the route the grown-ups approved, and check whether the parts you chose still fit your band.

## Start Here

Open your Budget Estimate page and circle any number you'd change now. That's the whole first step.

## Steps

Your first budget was a quick guess with two slices. Now you know your route, your nights in each city, your hotels and what an ordinary day costs. So your second pass can follow the route the grown-ups approved at Checkpoint 4, with any changes they made.

Round to easy numbers. A calculator is always fine, and a grown-up can do the math while you do the thinking.

1. **Hotels, city by city.** Fill in the hotel table in the Workspace, one row per overnight city. If a stop on your route is a region, use the town on its card from Session 21. If that card names no town, use the one you picked in Session 28. Use your nights from Checkpoint 4 and the cost per room from the card you recommended. If that card's cost says "per person", write the number of people staying in the Rooms column instead, so the hotel total is the cost times the people times the nights. If that card's cost row is empty, fill it in first, or ask an adult for a cost and whether it's per room or per person. A grown-up tells you how many rooms before you start. If your Session 33 note, or the grown-up's answer, says the number of us changes during a stay, give that city one row for each room count, or people count if it's per person, with its own nights.
2. **Food.** Take the medium food guess from your Session 38 table, and multiply it by your days on the ground: the total days in your Checkpoint 4 plan, minus each "- 1 flight day" mark on your Session 29 formula. A date spent in the air is part of the grown-ups' cost of getting there. Write it on your blank Budget Estimate page, which is your page for this pass. That guess already covers everyone, so on its Food row, cross out "per person" and "x ____ people". Does a place's price include meals, such as breakfast and dinner? Ask a grown-up. Then leave those meals out of your food for the nights you stay there.
3. **The other slices.** Do the same for local transit, activities and souvenirs, with the same days on the ground. Fill in the rows on that page that said "later". They're yours now. In each one's "How to figure it" box, write "per day x ____ days". On the activities row, cross out its "per ticket" formula first, and add your special pick's ticket once, counted the way Session 38 counts a ticket. Your long-distance rides from Session 38 stay out of your subtotal, because the grown-ups price those.

   **Someone with you for only part of the trip?** Your Session 38 guesses count everyone on every day. They use one price for each person. Take the parts of the trip from your Session 33 note, or from the grown-up's answer before this session. Count your traveler-days first: for each part of the trip, multiply how many of you are there by its days on the ground, and add the parts. Then, for food, divide your Session 38 guess by all of you, and multiply by your traveler-days. Do the same for local transit and activities, but first take out each price you wrote "once" beside. It's one price for the people it covers, so multiply it by your days on the ground, and add it back. If fewer or more of you are there on some days, check how many you'd need then. Souvenirs stay per day. A grown-up can do the math.
4. **Add it up.** Your subtotal is what the parts you chose come to. If a part still says "ask an adult" or "not sure yet", add up the rest, and write "not counting ___" beside what you added up, naming what's missing. That goes for your All hotels line too. The whole trip costs more than that, and the grown-ups work out the rest on their own page.
5. **Check against your band.** Match the units first, the way you did in Session 33, but count all five slices now. Band per day for the family? Divide your subtotal by your days on the ground, and compare that. Band per person, per day? Divide your subtotal by your traveler-days, and compare that. Your traveler-days are your travelers times your days on the ground, or the count from step 3. Your travelers are the number on your Trip-Basics card. If it says "not decided yet", count the travelers listed on the card instead. Band a hotel tier? Check each hotel's cost per night against it.
6. **Write one sentence.** Do the parts you chose fit the band? If your total leaves a part out, say so, such as "Without the hotel, it fits." If they don't, what would you change? Maybe fewer nights somewhere, a cheaper area, or one less paid ticket. Keep your special pick and its place. If a grown-up agrees to a change, go down your When the Plan Changes card for it.

If your special pick on your My Calls page costs more than the band, a grown-up tells you which of the three blocks it hit. Choose a new pick with them, and fix your pages by the special-pick row of your When the Plan Changes card. Here that includes this budget: take the blocked pick's ticket out of your activities slice, and add the new pick's ticket once, counted the way Session 38 counts a ticket. Then add up your subtotal, check it against your band again, and fix your sentence.

Sometimes nothing you try fits. Then "I recommend we change the trip, or wait until a better time" is a good, successful answer. Planners give that answer too.

## Artifact Created

## Stop Point

You are done when your budget summary matches the route the grown-ups approved at Checkpoint 4, with a hotel line for each city and the other slices filled in, your subtotal is added up, you've checked the matching piece against your band, and you've written one sentence about whether the parts you chose fit. If a part still says "ask an adult" or "not sure yet", your subtotal and your sentence both say what they leave out. If they don't, say what you'd change. "It doesn't fit, so I'd cut ___" is a finished, good answer. Your daily slices count only your days on the ground. If the number of us changes during a stay, that city has a row for each room count, or people count if it's per person. If someone is with you for only part of the trip, your food, local transit and activities use your traveler-days, and a price counted once is counted for each one you'd need on each day. If your special pick hit a block, you've chosen a new one with a grown-up, every page the card's special-pick row names is fixed, and your subtotal and band sentence use the new pick. "Ask an adult" is fine in any row you can't fill.

## Parent Notes

Before the session, tell your child how many rooms each overnight city needs, and on which nights that changes if someone comes for part of the trip. In that case, also say how many of you are there on which days, so your child can count traveler-days even without a Session 33 note. Review the rest after the session. Your child's second pass covers only the slices they control: hotels, food, local transit, activities and souvenirs. Your cost of getting there stays on your own page and is never copied into their running total. The [budget estimate](../../templates/budget_estimate.md) page says in as many words that getting there is the grown-ups' number, with nothing for your child to fill in. That is deliberate, and your child neither researches nor books flights.

**Your own sanity check is an addition.** Take their subtotal and add your cost of getting there for the whole party. If you fly or take the train, that is a per-person fare times the number of travelers, because their subtotal already covers everyone and a fare does not. If you drive, it is the drive's own costs. Once any flights are booked, update your check with the real fares; there is no flight line on your child's page to replace.

Read the result as a partial floor. It now covers all five slices plus getting there, and it still leaves out getting between cities, insurance, entry costs and phone service. So treat it as "are we in the right range?", and never as what the trip will cost. The [money and budget guidance](../../parent_guide/money_budget_guidance.md) explains the split.

Your child compares a matching piece of their estimate with the band, because the band is a rate or a tier and a subtotal is a whole-trip amount. The final budget is yours.

If the special pick on the My Calls page costs more than the band, name that block to your child and let them choose a new pick with you, from their attraction cards for places on the route, as in Session 26. Write "got it" beside the new pick on their My Calls page. The blocked pick needs no more research. Only the three blocks can change the pick.

If you agree to a change they suggest, your child carries it back with their When the Plan Changes card. If their honest answer is "change the trip or wait", receive it warmly. It is a valid, successful result, and it is the budget lesson working.
