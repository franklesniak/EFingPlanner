## What to notice

- Every entry is short. A few words in a box is a finished answer.
- The three trust levels are different, and each one has a short reason.
- Entry 2 was checked in a second place, the guidebook, and that check has its own date.
- Entries 1 and 3 leave the last two boxes empty. That's fine. You check a second source when a fact matters a lot.
- Ticket times can change, so entry 2 says to check again before booking.
- On your own log, write the web address in the box for it. These pretend pages leave web addresses out.
