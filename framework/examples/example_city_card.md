# Example: City Research Card

This is a filled copy of the [City Research Card](../templates/city_research_card.md). The pretend planner's first card was for Rome, the city a grown-up named. This second card is for Florence, a city the planner picked.

| Prompt | Your answer |
| --- | --- |
| City or region | Florence |
| Why people go there | Famous art and old buildings. The guidebook says a big time for art, called the Renaissance, started here. |
| One memorable fact | The giant dome on the cathedral is about 600 years old. It's made of millions of bricks. |
| Top sight 1 (star the ones you can't wait to see) | ★ The cathedral dome. You can climb up inside it. |
| Top sight 2 | ★ The old bridge with little shops on it (Ponte Vecchio) |
| Top sight 3 | The big art gallery (the Uffizi) |
| Food and culture notes | Lots of gelato shops. People often say "buongiorno" (good day) when they walk into a small shop. Later in the day they say "buonasera" (good evening). |
| Travel time from a likely nearby city | From Rome, about an hour and a half by fast train, the guidebook says. I'll check it with the map's Directions later. |
| How many days might we want here? | 2 or 3 days |
| Possible downsides (crowded? far? costly?) | Very crowded in summer. The dome climb has lots of stairs. Hotels in the center might cost more. |
| Best season fit (does it match our Checkpoint 1 season?) | Yes, I think so. Our season is fall, and the guidebook says fall is less crowded than summer. |
| Sources (also in my Source Log) | Library guidebook, pages 52 and 54; the cathedral's official website |
| Date checked | March 3 and March 12 |
| Planning assumption (something I'm assuming a grown-up should check) | We fly into Rome and take the train to Florence. |
| Why I'm using this assumption | Rome is my first card, and the guidebook says most people get to Florence by train. |
| What could change it? | The grown-ups might pick flights into a different city, or decide to drive. |
| Needs adult verification? (yes / no) | Yes |
| Final decision status (researching / shortlist / skip / save for future) | Shortlist |

## What to notice

- Two of the three sights have a star. Stars mark the ones the planner can't wait to see.
- The travel time is a rough span from the guidebook. The planner wrote down how to check it later.
- The planning assumption is marked "Yes" for a grown-up to check. Where the family flies in is the grown-ups' call.
- Each source has a date, so a grown-up knows how fresh it is.
- The status is "Shortlist". The planner can still move it later.
