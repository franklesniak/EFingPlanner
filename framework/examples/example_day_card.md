# Example: Daily Plan Card

This is a filled copy of the [Daily Plan Card](../templates/daily_plan_card.md). The pretend planner made one block card for each city-stay. This one is for Florence, the second and last stay of the trip.

## The per-day card (optional, later)

The pretend planner left this one blank. It's there for later, if the dates and travel plans get firm.

## What to notice

- The days start at 4, because the trip starts in Rome. Florence is the last stay, so the card has one more row, for the day the family heads home.
- Each day has one anchor. Day 4 is a moving day, so its anchor is the train ride. Day 7's anchor is the ride to where they leave from.
- Day 5 has a big morning and a light afternoon, so everyone gets a rest.
- On your own card, write the cost you worked out from your Session 38 medium total. These pretend pages leave prices out, so this card uses words like "higher" and "about the same".
- The card uses only day numbers and a season. Booked dates stay with the grown-ups.
- "Not decided yet" is a finished answer, like the sleep location here.
