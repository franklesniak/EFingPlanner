# Day Cards

## What goes in this folder

Your Daily Plan Cards, one card to a file. You make them in Session 41.

Make them only after Checkpoint 4, once the grown-ups have settled your route and how long the trip is. Cards made before that usually have to be redone.

The **block card** is the one to make: one card for each city-stay, with a row for each day in it. So one card can cover several days. A trip with one base has one block card. Per-day cards are extra, and only worth making once the dates and travel plans are firm.

## Where the blank comes from

The blank is the [Daily Plan Card template](../../../templates/daily_plan_card.md). It has the block card first and the per-day card after it. Copy it once for each card, and fill in the copy.

That template is the only blank. Leave it as it is. Keep no spare blank in this folder, so you always know which page is the empty one.

## How to name each file

Day cards use numbers, with a zero in front so they stay in order. Use numbers only, with no city names.

Block cards, one for each city-stay, in trip order:

- `block_01.md`
- `block_02.md`

Per-day cards, if you make them later:

- `day_01.md`
- `day_02.md`

## Keep it honest and open

Your plan is built in movable blocks, one per place. If a grown-up changes where you arrive or leave, you move a block, and the rest of your plan stays as it is. Nothing you wrote gets wasted.

"Roughly when" means a window, like "spring", "the second week" or "not decided yet". Keep exact booked dates off every card. "Not decided yet", "unknown" and "ask an adult" are complete answers in any box.

Opening times, ticket rules and prices change. A grown-up checks them again before the trip. If a booking changes kind, such as a walk-in place that now needs a timed ticket, use the "Something to book added, dropped, moved or changed?" row on your [When the Plan Changes card](../../../student_guide/when_the_plan_changes.md). Keep private details off every card, as the [privacy and safety](../../../docs/privacy_and_safety.md) page says.
