# Research

This part of your kit is where your research cards live while you work on them. Every card you fill in has a home in one of the folders below.

## The card folders

- [City cards](city_cards/README.md): one card for each city or region you research.
- [Attraction cards](attraction_cards/README.md): one card for each thing you might want to do.
- [Hotel cards](hotel_cards/README.md): one card for each place you might stay.
- [Restaurant cards](restaurant_cards/README.md): one card for each place or area to eat, if your family does the food sessions.
- [Day cards](day_cards/README.md): one card for each stretch of the trip, made after Checkpoint 4.

## One blank, many filled cards

The blank for every card lives in the repository, in `framework/templates/`. That's the only home of the blanks. Copy the blank once for each card you make, and save each copy as its own file in the right folder. Each folder's README names the blank to copy and shows how to name your files.

## One artifact, one home

Each card lives in one place only. A card you're still working on stays here. When a later page needs something from a card, carry the short version forward to that page. Keep one copy of each card, so you always know which one is right.

A link that leaves the kit opens a page in the repository. In a printed or pasted copy, find that page there.

## Keep it private

Your filled-in cards stay in your binder or your own Docs folder, away from any public repository. Keep private details off every card. The rules are on the [privacy and safety](../../docs/privacy_and_safety.md) page.
