# Restaurant Cards

## What goes in this folder

One filled restaurant research card for each place or area to eat, one card to a file. You make them in Session 37, from the food wish list you wrote in Session 36.

The food sessions are your family's choice. If your family skipped them, this folder stays empty, and your kit is complete without it. If you did them, Session 37 says how many cards are enough. A dining area counts as a card.

## Where the blank comes from

The blank is the [Restaurant Research Card template](../../../templates/restaurant_research_card.md). Copy it once for each place or area, and fill in the copy.

That template is the only blank. Leave it as it is. Keep no spare blank in this folder, so you always know which page is the empty one.

## How to name each file

Name each file for the place or the area. Use lowercase letters, put an underscore where a space would go, and end with `.md`.

Two examples of the shape, with made-up names:

- `lunch_spot_near_museum.md`
- `first_city_dining_area.md`

## Keep it honest and open

Write only what you found. If you're not sure a place needs a reservation, write "ask an adult". That's a finished answer, and so are "not decided yet" and "unknown".

Reservation rules, prices and whether a place takes cards all change. A grown-up checks them again before booking. Keep private details off every card, as the [privacy and safety](../../../docs/privacy_and_safety.md) page says.
