# Planner Mindset

How a good planner thinks. Read this once near the start, and come back to it when a session feels hard.

## Being off is normal

Your first guesses about time, cost, and energy will probably be off. Everyone's are, even grown-ups'. Being off is normal and expected. The point is to practice estimating and then compare, not to be exactly right. Every time you guess and then check, your guessing gets a little better.

## Good enough is good enough

<!-- density-exempt: X, not Y -- the spec says student-facing materials include this idea: the job is enough good information, not research forever -->
Your job is to collect enough good information to make a thoughtful recommendation, not to research forever. When you hit a stop point, you are done. Extra questions go in your [question parking lot](../templates/question_parking_lot.md), so you can stop searching and come back to them later.

## Frustration is information

Feeling frustrated in the middle of a session is normal. Noticing it -- and taking a break instead of quitting -- is a real planner skill. Stand up, get water, come back. The work will still be there.

## You are a real planner

The trip is real, and your recommendations matter. It's also low-stakes: grown-ups make every big decision, and "let's park this for later" is always okay. Your job is to help the grown-ups decide better. Nothing has to be perfect.
