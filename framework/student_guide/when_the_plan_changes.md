# When the Plan Changes

Plans change. A grown-up might move a place, change the nights, book the dates, or tell you your special pick can't work. That doesn't mean your work was wrong. You move a block and keep the rest, as [What I Decide](what_i_decide.md) explains.

When something changes, your earlier pages need to catch up. First, write the change where it happened, such as your itinerary draft or your Decision Record. Say where it changed, such as "changed at Checkpoint 5". Then go down this card. Do only the rows that fit your change, and fix only the pages you've already made. A page you haven't made yet will use the new plan when you get to it.

## What changed?

- **A day's plan?** Fix the day cards for the days that change, and your itinerary draft if you've made it.
- **Something to book added, dropped or moved?** Fix its row on your Reservation Watchlist, or fill in a new row the way Session 42 does. With booked dates, tell a grown-up, so they can fix its opening date.
- **A must-do dropped?** Cross it off your must-do list, and write why beside it. Put it on your cut list, if you've made one. Your special pick always stays.
- **A place dropped?** Cross off its must-dos the same way. Write "not on our route now" on its Hotel Comparison Card, if you've made one.
- **A place added?** It needs a place to stay. Make a Hotel Comparison Card for it, the way Session 35 does, or ask a grown-up for a cost per night and how many rooms.
- **A place or its nights changed?** Note it on your Checkpoint 4 route and trip-length recommendation. Cross out the old, and write the new beside it, with where it changed.
- **A new place to arrive or leave?** A grown-up writes it on your family's assumptions page, because later sessions read it there. Re-time that leg with the map's Directions, the way Session 28 does, and fix your travel days and nights. Then check your Session 29 formula for the way you travel:
  - **If you fly,** ask a grown-up whether each flight still lands on a later date. Fix your "- 1 flight day" marks to match, and use the row for a flight-day mark below.
  - **If you take a train or drive,** you have no flight-day marks to fix.
  - **However you travel,** ask whether the trip there or the trip home now takes only a few hours. Fix its ½ mark to match.
- **A flight-day mark added or taken away?** Each "- 1 flight day" mark is a date spent in the air. With booked dates, the total stays the same. So a new mark takes a night away, and a mark taken away gives one back. Ask a grown-up which night. With open dates, the total grows or shrinks by one day instead, and your nights stay. But the total can't go over your family's maximum trip length on your Trip-Basics card. If it's already there, a new mark takes a night away too. Note the change on your Checkpoint 4 recommendation, and go down this card again for any night that changes.
- **The season changed?** Note it on your Checkpoint 1 season recommendation, with where it changed. Then fix what you've made that uses the season: the "roughly when" row on your day cards, your Session 43 hot-weather check, the weather items on your Packing List, and the "When should we go?" line on your Final Presentation Outline.
- **A place, a night or a day changed?** If you've done Session 39, update your budget and check it against your band again. Someone with you for only part of the trip? Ask a grown-up who is there on the changed days, and count your traveler-days again. If you've done Session 43, run its pacing checks again for the days that changed.
- **Your special pick blocked?** A grown-up tells you which of the three blocks it hit. Choose a new pick with them, the way you did in Session 26. Pick it from your attraction cards for places on your route. Write it on your My Calls page in place of the blocked pick. The grown-up writes "got it" beside it. Write must-do on its card too. Put it on your must-do list, its day card, your draft and, if it needs booking, your watchlist. Take the blocked pick off them. It needs no more research. If your Session 39 budget counted the blocked pick's ticket, swap in the new one's, and check your band again.
- **Your dates or your travel got booked?** A grown-up writes the booked trip length on your Trip-Basics card, with "booked" beside it. Label each change below "changed after booking".
  - **A different season from your Checkpoint 1 pick?** Use the row for a changed season.
  - **A new place to arrive or leave?** Use that row above.
  - **The booked flights change your flight-day marks?** Use the row for a flight-day mark.
  - **The booked length isn't your Checkpoint 4 total?** Ask which nights change, and go down this card again for them.
  - **Made your Reservation Watchlist already?** It's an act-early list now. For each item, write how far ahead its booking opens in "When adults should check", the way Session 42 does. A grown-up turns it into the date.
- **Already put your binder together in Session 50?** Put each page you made or fixed behind its tab, in place of the old one.

You're done when every page this card names for your change is fixed. "Ask an adult" is a fine answer in any box you can't fill yet.
