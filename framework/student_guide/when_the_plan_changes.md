# When the Plan Changes

Plans change. A grown-up might move a place, change the nights, book the dates, or tell you your special pick can't work. That doesn't mean your work was wrong. You move a block and keep the rest, as [What I Decide](what_i_decide.md) explains.

When something changes, your earlier pages need to catch up. First, write the change where it happened, such as your itinerary draft or your Decision Record. Say where it changed, such as "changed at Checkpoint 5". Then use this card in two parts. First, do the rows under "What changed?" that fit your change. Then go down "Your plan pages" at the end. Fix only the pages you've already made. A page you haven't made yet will use the new plan when you get to it.

## What changed?

- **A day's plan?** Fix the day cards for the days that change. Then go down Your plan pages.
- **Something to book added, dropped, moved or changed?** For example, a place you could walk into now needs a timed ticket. Fix its row on your Reservation Watchlist, or fill in a new row the way Session 42 does. With booked dates, tell a grown-up, so they can fix its opening date. Then go down Your plan pages.
- **A must-do dropped?** Cross it off your must-do list, and write why beside it. Change its attraction card's group to skip or save for future. Put it on your cut list, if you've made one. Your special pick always stays. Then go down Your plan pages.
- **A place dropped?** Cross off its must-dos the same way. Write "not on our route now" on its Hotel Comparison Card and its restaurant cards, if you've made them. Has a grown-up filled in your "if I get separated" card for that place? Then it's torn up, and they fill in one for where you'll sleep instead. Then go down Your plan pages.
- **A place added?** It needs a place to stay and, if you've made your day cards, a day card, the way Session 41 makes one. Make a Hotel Comparison Card for it, the way Session 35 does, or ask a grown-up for a cost per night, per room or per person, and how many rooms or people. Then go down Your plan pages.
- **A different place to stay?** Mark the new Hotel Comparison Card as your pick. If you've done Session 39, fix its hotel line in the new card's unit: rooms, or people if it's per person. Has a grown-up filled in the lodging lines on your "if I get separated" card? Then they fill in a fresh card for the new place, and the old one is torn up, as Session 49 says. Then go down Your plan pages.
- **A different number of rooms?** A grown-up tells you how many rooms, and on which nights. Fix the room count on your Hotel Comparison Card, and the Rooms column of your Session 39 hotel table. Then go down Your plan pages.
- **A place or its nights changed?** Note it on your Checkpoint 4 route and trip-length recommendation. Cross out the old, and write the new beside it, with where it changed. Changed nights move the day numbers after them. So fix those day numbers wherever you wrote them. Then go down Your plan pages.
- **A new place to arrive or leave?** A grown-up writes it on your family's assumptions page, because later sessions read it there. Re-time that leg with the map's Directions, the way Session 28 does, and fix your travel days and nights. Then check your Session 29 formula:
  - **If you fly,** ask a grown-up whether each flight still lands on a later date. Fix your "- 1 flight day" marks to match, and use the row for a flight-day mark below.
  - **If you take a train or drive,** you have no flight-day marks. Did the way you travel change too? Use the row for how you travel.
  - **However you travel,** ask whether the trip there or the trip home now takes only a few hours. Fix its ½ mark to match.
  - **A place with a different time zone?** Some destinations have more than one. Ask whether the time difference changed. A grown-up fixes it on your Trip-Basics card. Then fix your jet-lag days on your Session 29 formula.
  - Then go down Your plan pages.
- **How you travel, or your home airport, changed?** For example, a drive becomes a train trip, or you'll fly after all. A grown-up fixes it on your Trip-Basics card, with the home airport and its code if you fly. Ask whether the trip there or home now takes only a few hours, and fix its ½ mark on your Session 29 formula. Flights added or gone, or landing on a different date? Use the row for a flight-day mark. Did the way you get between places change too? Re-time those rides with the map's Directions, the way Session 28 does. Then go down Your plan pages.
- **A flight-day mark added or taken away?** Each "- 1 flight day" mark is a date spent in the air. With booked dates, the total stays the same. So a new mark takes a night away, and a mark taken away gives one back. Ask a grown-up which night. With open dates, the total grows or shrinks by one day instead, and your nights stay. But the total can't go over your family's maximum trip length on your Trip-Basics card. If it's already there, a new mark takes a night away too. Note the change on your Checkpoint 4 recommendation, and go down this card again for any night that changes. Made your itinerary draft? Add or take away that "travel" row, first or last, and number the days after it again, the way Session 45 does. Then go down Your plan pages.
- **Your family's maximum trip length changed?** With open dates, a grown-up writes the new maximum on your Trip-Basics card. Is your Checkpoint 4 total now longer than that? Ask a grown-up which nights come off, and use the row for a place or its nights. Then go down Your plan pages.
- **The season changed?** Note it on your Checkpoint 1 season recommendation, with where it changed. Then go down Your plan pages.
- **A place, a night or a day changed?** If you've done Session 39, update your budget and check it against your band again. Someone with you for only part of the trip? Ask a grown-up who is there on the changed days, and count your traveler-days again. Then go down Your plan pages.
- **Your budget band changed?** A grown-up writes the new band on your family's assumptions page, and tells you. If you've done Session 39, check your budget against the new band, the way its step 5 does, and write your band sentence again, with where it changed. Does your special pick cost more than the new band? A grown-up tells you, and you use the row for a blocked special pick. Then go down Your plan pages.
- **A traveler's energy, walking or senses changed?** For example, someone now tires more easily, or finds crowds harder. A grown-up tells you. Fix that row on their traveler profile. Before Checkpoint 4, think about everyone's energy again, the way Session 29 does. After it, a shorter or gentler trip is a flag for the grown-ups, as Session 43 says. Then go down Your plan pages.
- **Who's coming changed?** A grown-up fixes the number of travelers and the roster on your Trip-Basics card, and tells you who is there on which days and how many rooms. Then fix what you've made:
  - **The people pages.** A new traveler gets a traveler profile, the way Session 02 does, and an answer on your Family Trip Goals poll, the way Session 03 does. Then check your balancing line again. For someone who isn't coming now, write "not coming" on their profile and their poll answer. Someone's part of the trip changed? Fix the whole-trip-or-part row on their profile.
  - **The counts.** Count again on your Session 33 part-trip note, the room count on your Neighborhood Comparison, the room-setup row on your Hotel Comparison Cards and the seating note on your restaurant cards. Count every Session 38 guess that counts people again (food, and any fare or ticket priced per person), with the day's totals. Count your Session 39 rooms (or people, where a place charges per person) and traveler-days again, and the food, local transit and activities slices, with the band checked again. Fix the souvenir amount if a grown-up changes it.
  - Then go down Your plan pages.
- **Your special pick blocked?** A grown-up tells you which of the three blocks it hit. Choose a new pick with them, the way you did in Session 26. Pick it from your attraction cards for places on your route. Write it on your My Calls page in place of the blocked pick. The grown-up writes "got it" beside it. Write must-do on its card too. Put it on your must-do list, its day card, your draft and, if it needs booking, your watchlist. Take the blocked pick off them. It needs no more research. If your Session 39 budget counted the blocked pick's ticket, swap in the new one's, counted the way Session 38 counts a ticket. Then check your band again. On the First Taste path, choose from the starred sights on your City Research Cards instead, as in Session 44, and swap it on your must-see list. Then go down Your plan pages.
- **Your dates or your travel got booked?** A grown-up writes the booked trip length on your Trip-Basics card, with "booked" beside it. Label each change below "changed after booking".
  - **A different season from your Checkpoint 1 pick?** Use the row for a changed season.
  - **Booked a different way to travel?** Use the row for how you travel.
  - **A new place to arrive or leave?** Use that row above.
  - **The booked flights change your flight-day marks?** Use the row for a flight-day mark.
  - **The booked length isn't your Checkpoint 4 total?** Ask which nights change, and go down this card again for them.
  - **Booked a different place to stay from your pick?** Use the row for a different place to stay.
  - **Made your Reservation Watchlist already?** It's an act-early list now. For each item, write how far ahead its booking opens in "When adults should check", the way Session 42 does. A grown-up turns it into the date.
  - Then go down Your plan pages.

## Your plan pages

Go down this list in order. For each page you've made, look at what it holds. If the change touches it, fix it there.

- **Your cover page** (Session 01): the travel party and home airport lines.
- **Your Destination Snapshot** (Session 10): the time difference and the "Getting there" row.
- **Your route notes** (Sessions 28 and 30): your route map notes, their travel times, and your transportation notes.
- **Your Session 29 formula**: its total, its ½ marks, its "- 1 flight day" marks and its jet-lag days.
- **Your must-do list and special pick**, on your My Calls page.
- **Your attraction cards**: each card's group, its "Ticket or reservation needed?" row and its price.
- **Your Session 33 part-trip note**: who is there on which days.
- **Your Neighborhood Comparison and Hotel Comparison Cards** (Sessions 34 and 35): the room count and room setup, and the nights beside them.
- **Your restaurant cards** (Session 37): the seating note.
- **Your budget** (Sessions 38 and 39): your daily cost table, your hotel table, your slices and your subtotal. Add it up again, and check your band sentence. Keep your Session 33 page as it is.
- **Your day rules** (Session 40): your first-day, last-day and moving-day rules, which follow your ½ marks.
- **Your day cards** (Session 41): each day's number, roughly when, where you'll sleep, its anchor and travel legs, its tickets row, its cost for everyone there, and its energy level.
- **Your Reservation Watchlist** (Session 42): each item's rows.
- **Your pacing review** (Session 43): run its checks again for each day the change touches. A traveler's profile counts only on the days they're there.
- **Your cut list and backup plans** (Session 44).
- **Your itinerary draft** (Session 45): each day's row, with its number, travel legs, costs and booking notes.
- **Your Packing List** (Session 48): its weather items, its plane items, and what a long walking day needs.
- **Your "if I get separated" card** (Session 49): a grown-up fixes its lodging lines.
- **Your Final Presentation Outline** (Session 51): if you haven't presented it yet, its Part 1 lines.
- **Your Checkpoint 6 packet** (Session 52): whose "one thing you'd love" the plan makes room for.
- **Your binder** (Session 50): put each page you fixed behind its tab, in place of the old one.

You're done when you've done the rows that fit your change and gone down Your plan pages. "Ask an adult" is a fine answer in any box you can't fill yet.
