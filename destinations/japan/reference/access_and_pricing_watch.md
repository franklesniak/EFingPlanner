# Access and Pricing Watch (Japan)

**Last reviewed:** September 2026

The items on this list are the facts most likely to have changed since each page in this pack was checked. So check each one again close to travel, even if you checked it earlier. Work down the list, and write the date beside each check.

Grown-ups own anything to do with entry, money and booking. You can help by flagging an item that looks out of date.

## What to check again

- **Timed entry and reservations** -- which attractions need a booked date or time now, and how far ahead booking opens. The [reservation examples](../session_inserts/42_reservation_examples.md) show the kinds of booking gates.
- **Permits** -- which activities need a permit now, such as climbing Mount Fuji, and what a permit costs. The [major cities page](major_cities.md) has the Mount Fuji area.
- **IC cards** -- which visitor cards exist and work now, including phone versions, and whether a phone bought outside Japan can add one. See [transportation basics](transportation_basics.md).
- **Rail passes** -- whether a pass pays for itself on this trip's route, at today's prices. See [transportation basics](transportation_basics.md).
- **Tourist and dual pricing** -- whether an attraction, a transit service or a venue charges visitors more than people who live there. See [money basics](money_basics.md).
- **Neighborhood access and photo limits** -- which areas have posted rules for visitors now. See [etiquette basics](etiquette_basics.md).
- **Child fares and age bands** -- the current ages and amounts, for the budget work. See [money basics](money_basics.md).
- **Entry authorization** -- what a US visitor needs to enter Japan now. Before paying any website, an adult checks the requirement and the official way to apply on the government's own page. See [adult logistics](adult_logistics.md).
- **Travel taxes** -- which taxes apply now, and how much they are. See [money basics](money_basics.md).
