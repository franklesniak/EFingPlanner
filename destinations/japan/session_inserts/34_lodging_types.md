# Destination Notes: Kinds of Places to Stay (Japan)

**Last reviewed:** September 2026

These are the kinds of places families stay in Japan. Use them to sort a night's options.

## The kinds of places

- **Western-style hotel** -- beds, a private bathroom and a front desk. It's the kind most like hotels in the United States.
- **Business hotel** -- a simple hotel with small rooms, often near a station.
- **Traditional inn** (*ryokan*) -- tatami-mat rooms, with futon beds laid out at night. It's usually priced per person, which is different from a hotel priced by the room.
- **Apartment-style or family hotel** -- bigger rooms, often with a small kitchen. It often lets a larger family share one space.
- **Licensed vacation rental** (*minpaku*) -- a house or apartment rented by the night.
- **Hostel or guesthouse** -- a simple place that often costs less, with shared rooms or shared bathrooms. Some have private family rooms.

## How many people fit in a room

That part belongs to the grown-ups. How many people a room holds, and what a bigger group plans around, is on your pack's [adult logistics page](../reference/adult_logistics.md). A grown-up checks it for each place.
