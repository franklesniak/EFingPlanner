# Destination Notes: Attraction Ideas (Japan)

**Last reviewed:** September 2026

Here are a few starter ideas for your attraction cards. Some are big, famous places. Others are everyday things that cost little. Each one is an idea to research, and the choosing is yours.

Each idea says what kind of visit it is, so you can see how it fits into a day. **Verify** means it needs a ticket, a booked time, or a fee that can change. Check it on its official website, and write down the date you checked.

## Big draws

- **Tokyo Disneyland and Tokyo DisneySea** (near Tokyo) -- a full day at one theme park. **Verify.**
- **The Ghibli Museum** (Tokyo area) -- a museum visit of a few hours. **Verify.**
- **teamLab** -- a company that makes walk-through digital art spaces. Its venues open, close and move, so check its official site for the ones open now. A visit is a walk through its rooms of digital art. **Verify.**

## Everyday things that cost little

- **A Shinkansen ride** -- something you do on the way between cities. On a route that already uses it, the ride is part of the travel the grown-ups plan and pay for. **Verify.**
- **Conveyor-belt sushi** -- a meal, with plates that ride past on a belt.
- **Goshuin stamps** -- a collection that grows during the trip, one page at each shrine or temple you visit. Your pack's major cities reference explains the custom. **Verify.**

## Kinds of visit

Every other idea fits one of these kinds too: a full day, a half-day, a timed visit, a meal, a quick stop on the way past, or a collection that grows. Use them for the ideas you find on the full list.

## Booking ahead

Ideas marked **Verify** may need booking ahead, and grown-ups do the booking. They come back later, when you make your reservation watchlist.
