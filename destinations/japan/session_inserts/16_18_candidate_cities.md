# Destination Notes: Deep-Dive Cities (Japan)

**Last reviewed:** September 2026

These are three big cities that first trips to Japan often include. Each one is a city to research. You and your family make the choices.

## The candidate cities

- **Tokyo** -- the capital, a huge city made of many neighborhoods. **Tokyo is the first-trip anchor.** Most first trips keep it, so it's usually your City A.
- **Kyoto** -- the old capital, with temples, shrines, gardens and quiet traditional streets.
- **Osaka** -- a lively city known for its food, and an easy base for day trips nearby.

Kyoto and Osaka sit close together. Either one could be your City B or City C. So could a big city you found on your own.
