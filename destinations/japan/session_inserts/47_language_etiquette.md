# Destination Notes: Language and Etiquette (Japan)

**Last reviewed:** September 2026

These are the words and manners for your quick sheet. They're the everyday ways things are done in Japan. People are glad when a visitor tries them.

## Words to try

| To say | In Japanese | Say it like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hello | Konnichiwa | kohn-nee-chee-wah |
| Thank you | Arigatou gozaimasu | ah-ree-gah-toh goh-zye-mahss |
| Excuse me, or sorry | Sumimasen | soo-mee-mah-sen |
| Please (when asking for something) | Onegaishimasu | oh-neh-guy-shee-mahss |
| Yes | Hai | hi |
| No | Iie | ee-eh |
| This one, please (point to it on the menu) | Kore o kudasai | koh-reh oh koo-dah-sigh |
| The bill, please | Okaikei onegaishimasu | oh-kye-kay oh-neh-guy-shee-mahss |

Give each part of a word about the same weight. For your own restaurant phrase, pick one from your pack's [language basics page](../reference/language_basics.md). It has a few more words, and it shows how Japanese writing looks.

## Manners worth knowing

- **Quiet on trains and buses.** People talk softly, and phones stay on silent.
- **Shoes indoors.** People take their shoes off in homes and traditional inns, and in some restaurants and temple halls. A step up at the door is the sign.
- **Trash.** Public bins are hard to find, so people carry a small bag and take their trash with them.
- **Shrines, temples and historic sites.** Walk calmly, speak softly and follow the posted signs.
- **No tipping.** The price on the bill is what you pay, at restaurants, in taxis and at hotels. The [etiquette basics page](../reference/etiquette_basics.md#handing-over-money) has more on handing over money.
- **Cash.** How people pay is worth checking before the trip, and grown-ups handle getting cash. Your pack's [money basics page](../reference/money_basics.md) has the details.
- **Signs and photos.** Some places limit photos, and a few post fines. Follow the signs. Always ask before you take a photo of a person.
- **Shared baths.** Hot-spring baths (*onsen*) and public baths (*sento*) have their own rules. Your pack's [etiquette basics page](../reference/etiquette_basics.md) explains them. Whether you take part is a grown-up's call.

The etiquette basics page has more on these manners. The money basics page has more on cash.
