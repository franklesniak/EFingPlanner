# Destination Notes: Reservations and Timed Entries (Japan)

**Last reviewed:** September 2026

These are some things in Japan that are booked for a set date. Grown-ups do all the booking. Each one says roughly how far ahead to book, and what kind of gate it has. Use them as examples of what to look for on your own list.

## Three kinds of gate

- **A fixed window** -- booking opens a set time before the date.
- **A sell-out** -- booking opens, and the popular dates fill fast. Some fill within minutes.
- **A lottery** -- a grown-up applies for dates, and a random draw decides who gets tickets.

One place can have more than one kind.

## Examples

Horizons last checked: September 2026.

- **The Ghibli Museum** (Tokyo area) -- about a month ahead. A whole month of tickets goes on sale at once, each for a set date and entry time. Popular days sell out fast. Tickets are sold only in advance.
- **Tokyo Disneyland and Tokyo DisneySea** -- about two months ahead. Each ticket is for one date, and busy dates can sell out.
- **teamLab** -- no fixed opening date, so grown-ups book as soon as the date is firm. Busy days can sell out a few weeks ahead, and holidays sooner. teamLab is a company whose art venues open, close and move, so check its official site for the ones open now. Each ticket is for a set date and entry time.
- **Super Nintendo World at Universal Studios Japan** (Osaka) -- a few months ahead, or on the day. On busy days, entry to this area is by timed ticket. A paid pass that includes an entry time can sell out. Free entry times are given out on the day, through the park's app, and they go fast.
- **The Nintendo Museum** (near Kyoto) -- a few months ahead. It uses a lottery: a grown-up applies for dates, and a draw decides.
- **Reserved seats on long-distance trains** -- about a month ahead. Seats go on sale at a fixed point before the travel date, and holiday trains fill up.

Each of these can change. Check each one again on its official site close to the trip.

## If your dates are already booked

Each horizon gives a rough idea of how far ahead to book. The official site gives the exact rule, where it has one. A grown-up turns it into a date in their own calendar.
