# Destination Notes: Other Places Research (Japan)

**Last reviewed:** September 2026

Here's a menu of places beyond the big cities. It has three kinds of places. Each one is a place to research, and the choosing is yours.

## Well-known places

- **Hiroshima** -- a peace park and a museum about the city's history.
- **Nikko** -- brightly carved shrines in a forest, north of Tokyo.
- **Kanazawa** -- a famous garden and old samurai streets.
- **Takayama** -- an old wooden town in the mountains.
- **The Mount Fuji area** -- lakes and big mountain views.
- **Fukuoka** -- ramen and evening food stalls, in the south.
- **Koyasan** -- a temple town on a mountain top.
- **Naoshima** -- a small island full of art.

## Places kids often love

- **Nara** -- deer that wander a big park, near Kyoto and Osaka.
- **Miyajima** -- deer on the streets, and a shrine gate that stands in the sea.
- **Hakone** -- pirate-ship boats on a lake, and hot springs, near Tokyo.
- **Hokkaido** -- deep snow in winter, and flower farms in summer.
- **Okinawa** -- beaches, coral reefs and a huge aquarium.

## Everyday places that cost little

- **Yanaka** -- an old Tokyo neighborhood of small shops and quiet temples. Walking around is free.
- **Kawagoe** -- an old merchant town near Tokyo, with a lane of candy shops.
- **Uji** -- a green-tea town beside a river, near Kyoto.

## Where to find out more

More about every place on this menu is in your pack's [major cities reference](../reference/major_cities.md). A place you find yourself belongs on the menu too.
